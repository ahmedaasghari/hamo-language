# Hamo Grammar Rules

Hamo eliminates complex verb conjugations, gendered nouns, and irregular forms. Meaning is derived through word order and simple pre-modifiers.

## 1. Sentence Order: Subject - Verb - Object (SVO)
$$\text{Subject (Who)} + \text{Verb (Action)} + \text{Object (What)}$$
* Example: **Mano peko kani** (*Person works on thing*).

## 2. Time Aspect Markers
Place the time marker **before** the verb:
* **Tami** = Past marker (Completed action)
* **Tila** = Present marker (Ongoing action / default)
* **Taki** = Future marker (Intended/upcoming action)
* Example: **Li tami hawa** (*You ate*).

## 3. Modifiers (Questions & Negation)
* **Kora** (Question Particle): Placed at the very start of a sentence.
  * Example: **Kora li peko?** (*Are you working?*)
* **Nopa** (Negation Particle): Placed immediately before the word it negates.
  * Example: **Mi nopa peko** (*I am not working*).

## 4. Compound Modifiers (Adjectives / Adverbs)
Adjectives follow the noun they modify. Adverbs follow the verb.
* **Mano kepo** = Good person (*Person + Good*).
* **Peko kepo** = Work well (*Work + Good*).
